# Form drawings

The looping GIFs in this folder are derived from exercise drawings by
**Everkinetic** (Greg Priday), published at
<http://db.everkinetic.com> and mirrored on
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Weight_training_diagrams)
under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0**
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>).

What changed: each drawing pair was placed on a white background, scaled
into a 340×340 square, and saved as a short looping GIF (about 0.7 seconds
per pose). The poses themselves were not redrawn.

These GIFs are shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 3.0.
Attribute Everkinetic and link the license. The rest of this repository
(the tracker code) is separate from these drawings.

Source filenames are listed in `scripts/build-demos.py`.
